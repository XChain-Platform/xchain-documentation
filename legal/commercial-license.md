## Do I need a commercial license?

**You do NOT need one** (AGPL is enough) if any of these is true:

- You use XChain for personal, hobby, research, or educational purposes.
- You run XChain **unmodified**, for any purpose, including inside a company.
- You modify XChain and you are willing to **release your modified source code** to everyone who interacts with it over a network, under the AGPL.

**You DO need a commercial license** if:

- You want to **modify XChain and keep your modifications private** (closed-source), while running it as a service or product, internal or external.
- You want to **embed or build XChain into a proprietary product** without that product becoming subject to the AGPL's source-disclosure requirements.
- Your legal or compliance policy **prohibits AGPL-licensed software**, and you need different terms.

In short: **the AGPL is free, but it requires you to share your changes. The commercial license is the paid way to keep your changes private.**

## Why dual licensing?

XChain Platform is, and will remain, genuinely open source under the AGPL: anyone can read, run, modify, and share it for free. The commercial license exists so that companies who want to build **private, proprietary** value on top of XChain contribute back to the project that makes it possible. This funds ongoing development and keeps the open-source version healthy for everyone.
